ALGORITHM TRAINING

Learn algorithms effectively

  • Recognize first. Study the top stickers and side-color blocks before you start turning. Knowing the case early prevents pauses mid-algorithm.
  • Choose comfortable finger tricks. If an algorithm causes repeated regrips, compare an alternate version and practice the one that flows naturally for you.
  • Chunk the sequence. Break a long algorithm into short move groups, repeat each group smoothly, then join the groups together.
  • Drill both recognition and execution. Mix the case with other cases so you learn to identify it, not just repeat it from a memorized setup.
  • Track progress with the timer. Practice one case at a time, then return to full solves and watch for recognition pauses.

ALGORITHM NOTATION

Reading algorithm moves

Uppercase face letters turn one face clockwise when viewed directly. A prime (') reverses a turn, and 2 means a half turn. Lowercase face letters turn that face and its middle slice together; M turns the middle slice between L and R. Lowercase x, y, and z rotate the whole cube.

R Turn the right face

R' Turn the right face backward

R2 Turn the right face halfway

r Wide right turn

M Middle slice turn

x Whole-cube rotation like R

y Whole-cube rotation like U

z Whole-cube rotation like F
