THE METHOD

The four stages

01

Cross

Match the four edge pieces around one center, including their side colors. Solve the cross on the bottom so you can see the rest of the puzzle.

Shortcut: Plan the whole cross during inspection, then try to solve it without rotating the cube.

02

F2L

Pair each first-layer corner with its matching middle-layer edge, then insert the pair into its slot. Repeat for all four slots.

Shortcut: Learn intuitive pair building before memorizing lots of cases. While inserting one pair, look for the next.

03

OLL

Orient the last-layer pieces so the top face is one color. You can start with two-look OLL: orient the edges, then orient the corners.

Shortcut: Learn two-look OLL first; expand to full OLL as you get comfortable recognizing cases.

04

PLL

Move the last-layer pieces into their correct positions while keeping the top face oriented. Two-look PLL solves corners first, then edges.

Shortcut: Learn two-look PLL first, then add full PLL cases gradually and practice recognizing them before turning.

STEP-BY-STEP

How to solve F2L

Keep your cross on the bottom. F2L is four repeats of the same idea: pair one corner with its matching edge, then insert that pair into its slot.

  1. Choose an open slot. Look at the two center colors beside it. Find the corner with those colors and the cross color, then find the edge with the same two side colors.
  2. Bring both pieces to the top layer. Use U turns and small setup moves. If a target piece is trapped in the slot, take it out without disturbing the rest of the solved cross.
  3. Pair the pieces above their slot. Match their side colors and join the corner and edge while keeping the cross intact. The exact setup depends on which stickers face up or out.
  4. Insert the pair. Turn the pair over its matching slot and use a short insertion. Repeat for the other three slots; avoid rotating the whole cube when a U turn will do.
Useful triggers to practice

R U R' and U' L' U L are common right- and left-side triggers. They are building blocks, not one-size-fits-all F2L solutions—the setup depends on the case.

LAST LAYER · STEP 1

How to solve OLL

With the first two layers solved, make the top face all one color. A beginner-friendly route is two-look OLL: orient the top edges, then orient the top corners.

  1. Orient the edges. Look only at the four top edges. Use U turns to line up the pattern, then use the matching edge-orientation algorithm. The line case should be held horizontally.
  2. Orient the corners. Keep the top color facing up and identify the corner pattern. Learn Sune and anti-Sune first; use U turns to set up a case, apply the matching sequence, and check the top again.
  3. Confirm the top face. All four top edges and all four top corners should show the top color. The side colors may still be out of order—that is what PLL fixes.
Edge line F R U R' U' F' Hold the top-layer line horizontally.
Sune R U R' U R U2 R' A common corner-orientation case.
Anti-Sune R U2 R' U' R U' R' The opposite Sune corner pattern.

These are starter cases, not the complete OLL set. Learn the case recognition and setup for each algorithm; do not apply one sequence to every pattern.

LAST LAYER · STEP 2

How to solve PLL

Once the top face is oriented, permute the last-layer pieces so each side color matches its center. In two-look PLL, solve corners first, then edges.

  1. Permute the corners. Use the side colors to spot a correctly placed corner or a matching pair. Turn U to set up the case, then use the matching A-perm direction to cycle the corners.
  2. Permute the edges. When the corners match their centers, check whether the top edges need a clockwise or counterclockwise cycle. Set up with U and use the matching U-perm.
  3. Align the top layer. Finish with a U turn if needed. Every side should now be a single color.
Aa-perm · corners x R' U R' D2 R U' R' D2 R2 x' Cycles three corners one way.
Ab-perm · corners x R2 D2 R U R' D2 R U' R x' Cycles three corners the other way.
Ua-perm · edges R U' R U R U R U' R' U' R2 Cycles the top edges one way.
Ub-perm · edges R2 U R U R' U' R' U' R' U R' Cycles the top edges the other way.

These are starter PLL algorithms. Use the case pattern and U setup to choose a direction; the listed sequences are not interchangeable for every last-layer pattern.

BUILD BETTER HABITS

Speed shortcuts

  • Reduce pauses. Turn at a steady pace and use F2L to search for your next pair while the current pair is going in.
  • Keep the cube steady. Prefer finger tricks and efficient solutions over unnecessary whole-cube rotations or regrips.
  • Practice one stage at a time. Slow solves focused on cross planning or F2L lookahead build better habits than rushing every solve.
  • Learn algorithms in steps. Get comfortable with two-look OLL and PLL before taking on the full sets.

READING MOVES

Basic notation

Letters name the face you turn, viewed straight on: R right, L left, U up, D down, F front, and B back.

R Turn the right face clockwise

R' Turn the right face counterclockwise

R2 Turn the right face 180°

x Rotate the whole cube like an R turn

y Rotate the whole cube like a U turn

z Rotate the whole cube like an F turn
